# Info

RtDLegends adds a full set of new weapons, armor, and randomized dungeons to hunt through, all tied together with a hidden lore thread for anyone who wants to dig deeper.

* All weapons have special secondary attacks
* All RtD bows have a 45% chance for your arrow to proc a unique explosion
* Adds a multitude of weapons & armor to choose from
* Adds custom set effects to many Vanilla sets that don't already have one
* 8 unique randomized dungeons
* Unique lore tied to weaponry and amours. 
* Random custom boss vegvisirs are now inside the custom dungeons
* All combat styles start out simple, you won't be swinging anything fancy right away
* Defeating **Eikthyr** unlocks the first real stage of gear
* From there, progression is gated by biome:
  * You'll need a **unique boss-drop item** from that biome's boss
  * You'll also need the **previous tier** of the weapon or armor you're upgrading
* Assassins, Archers, and Warriors will need to seek out custom dungeons, slay powerful corrupted bosses, and farm unique biome-specific metals to keep climbing.
* This keeps melee and ranged progression in step with the world's difficulty curve, so you can't easily outscale the content early on
* Combine all RtD mods to have a fully developed Deep North!

# Translations

* Currently English, Chinese, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Polish, Russian, Spanish, Ukrainian & Swedish are supported.
* Is your language not listed? Join my discord and feel free to make request. 
* Want to make your own JSON? Place it inside the mods plugin `Asset` Folder.
